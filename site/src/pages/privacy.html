Privacy policy

Effective November 25, 2026. This policy covers Vibe CMS, a product of dbVantage (“we”): this website, the Vibe CMS engine and Claude Code plugin, and the Vibe CMS connector for ChatGPT, Claude and other AI assistants.

The short version

  • A Vibe CMS website runs in its owner’s own Cloudflare account. Its pages, editor accounts, images, drafts and form entries are stored there, not with us.
  • The Claude Code plugin runs on your computer and talks only to your Cloudflare account. It sends nothing to us.
  • The connector for AI assistants stores only what it needs to reach your site: which sites you connected, an encrypted key for each, and its own sign-in tokens. Page content passes through it to answer a request and is not stored.
  • We don’t sell personal information, show ads, or use your content to train AI models.

1. Vibe CMS websites

When a developer installs Vibe CMS on a site, the engine runs as a Cloudflare Worker in the site owner’s Cloudflare account and stores everything in that account’s D1 database and R2 bucket: page edits and their history, editor names, email addresses and password hashes or sign-in links, images and files, form entries, and records of AI-assistant connections (a hashed key, its name, its permission level, and a count of recent requests used for rate limiting, cleared after a day). The site owner controls this data and is responsible for it toward their own visitors. We cannot see it.

Optional features send data to providers the site owner chooses and configures: Resend (emailed invites, sign-in links and form alerts), Cloudflare Turnstile (form spam checks) and Cloudflare Workers AI (writing help, run in the owner’s account).

2. The Claude Code plugin

The plugin is a set of instructions and scripts that Claude Code runs on your computer. It reads and changes files in your project and runs Wrangler, Cloudflare’s command-line tool, against your Cloudflare account. It has no telemetry and sends no data to us. What Claude Code itself sends to Anthropic is covered by Anthropic’s policies.

3. The connector for ChatGPT, Claude and other assistants

The connector lets an AI assistant read and edit a Vibe CMS site after the site’s editor signs in and approves it. To do that we store:

When an assistant asks for a page or makes an edit, the page text and the requested change pass through the connector to your site and back to the assistant. We don’t store that content or log it. We never receive your conversations with the assistant, only the tool requests the assistant sends.

Changes made through the connector are recorded in the site’s own history, under the connection’s name, like any editor’s change. A connection approved for drafts only cannot publish.

4. This website

This site (cms.dbvantage.com) runs on Vibe CMS. If you fill in the early-access or support form, we receive what you type (name, email and message) and use it only to reply to you and to run the early-access programme. Form entries are kept for 12 months, then deleted. The site has no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. Signed-in editors get a session cookie that is needed to edit the site.

5. Who receives data

We use Cloudflare to host this website and the connector, and Resend to send email. They process data on our behalf under their own data-processing terms. We share data with others only when the law requires it, or to protect the security of the service and its users. We don’t sell or rent personal information.

6. Your choices

  • Disconnect a site at any time by asking the assistant to disconnect it, or have a site admin revoke the connection under Editors > AI assistants. Disconnecting deletes the stored key at once. Removing the connector in the assistant’s own settings stops the assistant from using it.
  • Ask us to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you through the support page. We reply within 30 days.
  • For data stored on a Vibe CMS site, contact that site’s owner. They hold it in their own account.

7. Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Connection keys are encrypted at rest, sign-in tokens and site keys are stored as hashes, and sites check every request against the key’s permission level. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach that affects your data we will tell you without undue delay.

8. Children

Vibe CMS is a tool for running websites and is not directed at children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect their information.

9. Changes and contact

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top, and for significant changes we will say so on this site before they take effect. Questions: use the support page.
