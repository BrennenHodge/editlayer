Common questions

An editor lost their invite link or password

A site admin can send a new link from Editors in the editor. Developers can run the invite-editor skill in Claude Code.

ChatGPT or Claude says it can’t publish

The connection was approved for drafts only. Publish from the editor, or disconnect and connect again choosing Drafts and publishing.

How do I disconnect an assistant?

Remove the connector in the assistant’s settings, or have a site admin revoke it under Editors > AI assistants. Revoking stops it immediately.

How do I undo a change?

Open the page in the editor and use History. Every change, including ones made by an assistant, can be restored from there.
