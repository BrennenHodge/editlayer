Questions

About plans

Who pays, me or my client?

Either. Many developers include the editor in their monthly care plan; others have the client hold the plan for their own site.

What do I need besides Editlayer?

A Cloudflare account for the site, and Claude Code (or a terminal) to install it. ChatGPT or Claude only if you want AI editing.

What if I stop paying?

Your site keeps working: its pages and published edits stay where they are, in your Cloudflare account. Editing is what stops.
