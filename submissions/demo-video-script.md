# Demo video (about 2 minutes)

Record the screen in ChatGPT (or Claude) with the reviewer account on demo.editlayer.co. No voice-over needed;
on-screen text is enough. Upload unlisted (YouTube or Loom) so reviewers can open it without signing in, and put
the link in both review forms.

1. **Add the connector (25 s).** chatgpt.com/plugins > + > Add custom MCP server, URL
   `https://mcp.editlayer.co/mcp`, OAuth. Show the "Let ChatGPT edit your website" page, type
   `demo.editlayer.co`, sign in, choose **Save drafts and publish**, Allow.
2. **Look around (15 s).** In a chat: "What pages does my Juniper Café website have?" Show the answer listing Home,
   Menu and Visit.
3. **Small edit (25 s).** "Change the Saturday opening hours on the home page from 8am – 2pm to 9am – 3pm. Don't
   publish yet." Show the reply saying it's a draft. Open demo.editlayer.co in another tab: the live page still
   says 8am – 2pm.
4. **Rewrite and SEO (25 s).** "Rewrite the About us paragraph so it sounds warmer and mentions the window seats,"
   then "Give the Menu page a better Google title and description."
5. **Review and publish (20 s).** "Show me what's waiting, then publish the home page." Show the confirmation, then
   refresh the live page: Saturday now says 9am – 3pm.
6. **Safety (10 s).** Show the site's editor: History lists the change under the connection's name, and
   Editors > AI assistants can turn the connection off.
